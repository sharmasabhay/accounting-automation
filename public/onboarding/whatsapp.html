Connect your WhatsApp Business account

Authorize Omakase as a partner on your WhatsApp Business account. This lets us receive your purchase orders and invoices over WhatsApp and send you order confirmations and payment updates — all from your own business number.

  • Log in with the Facebook account that manages your business
  • Select (or create) your WhatsApp Business account and phone number
  • Grant Omakase access to manage messaging on your behalf

You are granting access only to your WhatsApp Business messaging — never your personal account. You can revoke access anytime from Meta Business Settings → Partners.
